## 🌟 What is Friends Party?

Have you ever organized a weekend barbecue, a rooftop dinner, a birthday hangout, or a holiday road trip with your friends—only to spend the next few days doing confusing math on paper, hunting down lost receipts, or having awkward conversations trying to figure out who owes who?

**Friends Party takes care of that completely.**

It is a simple, friendly mobile and web app made specifically for groups of friends. Whether you are hanging out for an evening or planning a big reunion, Friends Party keeps track of your shopping lists, pools advance money, divides the final bill fairly, and makes settling up painless and completely transparent.

No calculators, no messy spreadsheets, and zero awkwardness.

---

## ✨ Why You and Your Friends Will Love It

- 🎯 **Automatic Fair Math**: As soon as you finish shopping and publish your event, the app instantly calculates what each person pays down to the exact coin.
- 💵 **Advance Money Credited Instantly**: If friends chip in cash before shopping begins, their advance money is recorded. The app credits it against their bill so nobody ever pays twice.
- 🤝 **Automatic Refunds**: If a friend paid more advance cash than their final share of the bill, the app creates an instant refund request so the cashier can hand their change back.
- 🛒 **Protected Shoppers**: Friends who volunteer to do the physical shopping using group money won't have those items counted as their personal debt.
- 🎁 **Generous Donors & Sponsors**: If a friend wants to treat everyone or donate money toward snacks, their donation is deducted directly from the total bill, reducing costs for everyone. Donors can also choose whether to pay their normal meal share or be completely exempt.
- 🎈 **Honorary Guests**: Invite out-of-town guests, teachers, or special visitors without dividing the party bill onto them.
- 🛡️ **Pause Payments Anytime**: If a bill needs a quick review or an item is being swapped, the party organizer can pause payments with one tap until everything is ready.
- 📶 **Works Anywhere, Even Offline**: Hanging out on a remote rooftop, camping in the forest, or enjoying a beach campfire with no cellular network? The app works offline and updates smoothly the moment you get back online.
- 🔄 **One-Tap Updates**: Check for new features and updates directly inside the app without needing to search online.

## 🧭 How It Works (Step-by-Step)

Using Friends Party is as easy as 1-2-3:

```
  1. Create a Circle   ➔   2. Plan an Event   ➔   3. Assign Roles   ➔   4. Settle Up Fairly
   Add your friends        List shopping items      Cashier, Donors,         Everyone knows
   into a group            and who buys what        Advance Payers & Guests  what they owe or get back
```

### Step 1: Create Your Circle
- Open the app and create a circle for your group of friends (for example, *"College Buddies"*, *"Rooftop Hangouts"*, or *"Weekend Explorers"*).
- Add your friends to the circle so everyone is connected for upcoming events.

### Step 2: Plan Your Event & Shopping List
- Tap **Plan a Party**, choose a name (like *"Winter Barbecue"* or *"Game Night"*), and set the date.
- Add items to your shopping list (meat, drinks, charcoal, ice cream, plates) with quantities.
- Assign a friend to pick up each item so nobody buys the same thing twice.

### Step 3: Set Roles Easily
- **Appoint 1 Cashier**: Choose one trusted friend who will hold the pool money, confirm payments, and hand out refunds.
- **Advance Payers**: Record cash given to the cashier before shopping starts.
- **Donors & Sponsors**: If someone wants to treat the group with a donation, enter their gift. If they also want to share the meal bill, simply check *"Pay Bill Share"*.
- **Guests**: Add friends who are invited for free.

### Step 4: Finalize & Settle Up
- When the shopping is done, tap **Publish**.
- The app automatically calculates everyone's exact share.
- Check your balance card:
  - 🟢 **All Clear**: You are completely settled up!
  - 🔴 **Payment Due**: The exact amount you owe the cashier.
  - 🟡 **Refund Ready**: The exact cash amount waiting for you to collect from the cashier.
- Pay the cashier using cash or your favorite digital payment method, tap to send proof, and the cashier marks you all clear.

## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What happens if a friend gives cash before shopping?**  
A: Enter them as an Advance Payer with their amount. The app deducts their advance contribution from their final bill. If their advance payment was larger than their bill share, they get a refund.

**Q: Can a donor also pay their share of the meal?**  
A: Yes! When adding a donor, simply check the *"Pay Bill Share"* box. Their donation reduces the bill for everyone, and they also share the remaining cost like a regular participant.

**Q: Can we use the app without an internet connection?**  
A: Yes. You can record items, view parties, and check off shopping while offline. When your phone reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data, all your changes save automatically.
